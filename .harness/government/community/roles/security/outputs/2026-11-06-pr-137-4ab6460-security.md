## S001: publishing permission is not answer authority

The PM clarified that a flagged non-holder may publish an output, but --answers, request finalization and the associated forced wake require the writer to be one of:

1. The request's recorded holder.
2. A live holder of the request's role.
3. The request project's prime.

The current handler loads the request by ID without checking these authorities. Its writer_holds_role flag uses the output's context and is informational. The output is committed, the request is stamped answered and its stored requester is notified even when the writer is outside all three permitted cases.

This permits forged request completion and suppresses outstanding-work/time-limit escalation. It also exposes the associated forced-cold-wake path to an ineligible answerer. Prerequisites are a legitimate resolved local seat and a known request ID, not paired-machine access, credential forgery or database corruption.

The finding is grounded in the PM's explicit authority ruling, not an invented holder-only output restriction. The existing machine-wide bearer model is unchanged; the missing check is the API's logical request-finalization authority.

## Existing passing CI is a concrete witness

In crates/daemon/tests/output_http.rs:145-175, pij-writer is the writer and the sole live reviewer assignment belongs to pij-holder. At 224-243 the request belongs to fleet/reviewer with an empty instance and recorded holder pij-holder.

Project creation at 199-209 supplies no --prime. Project creation does not make its creator prime: http/governance.rs:713-729 obtains prime_id only from the optional flag, independently of created_by, and store/governance.rs:35-44 preserves None.

Thus pij-writer is the project creator, but is not its prime, the recorded holder or a live holder of the requested role. Its parent pij-pm supplies no authority under the ruling.

The test at output_http.rs:419-444 nevertheless submits the answer as pij-writer and asserts answered_at and notification. Independently inspected JUnit confirms that acceptance case passed. This proves finalization/notification admission through the real handler, not an actual cold-session wake. The inherited mark_role_request_answered helper checks ID and an unset timestamp; it does not authorize the output writer.

## Required repair

Resolve eligibility from the stored request, not the output's declared context or its non-holder flag. Before any writer commit, stamp or notification, admit exactly the three authorities in the ruling. Otherwise return a named refusal explaining how to get assigned or write without --answers. Do not silently downgrade an unauthorized answer into a published output.

Keep flagged non-holder publication without --answers. Preserve recorded-holder and project-prime authority rather than replacing the rule with current-holder-only access. Project creation alone is not prime designation.

Regression coverage should refuse an ineligible answerer without document/store/stamp/notification side effects and retain all three positive authority cases. No additional output/request metadata-equality rule or universal suppression of later revised outputs is required by this finding.
