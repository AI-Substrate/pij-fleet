## Audit and answered-state limits

Ordering is stamp once, resolve live recipient, await mandatory cold-wake audit, then deliver. Audit failure prevents dispatch. Audit actor is daemon pij-bg, with fixed reasons and request-derived message IDs, not an impersonated requester. Correlation uses those IDs; in_reply_to remains unset.

Stamps suppress duplicate attempts, not proof of successful delivery or model completion. A failed delivery can leave a stamp. Answered rows leave subsequent open queries, but answer suppression is not atomic with a round's already-read snapshot. The answer-stamping helper introduces no new HTTP authorization surface at this head.
