Such a window persists and stays visible (`a_foreign_window_in_pij_stays_listed_and_outlives_the_control_client`); the resolution is pinned by `an_untargeted_outside_command_resolves_to_pij_after_the_control_attach`. | R-PRIME-4 | timeout and replacement (review S4) | On a reply timeout the read forks and the client is replaced, so later reads stop waiting on a hung client. The queue, not the replacement, is what keeps a late reply from the next caller: the timed-out waiter's sender stays queued and absorbs it. A waiter clears `state.live` only if it is still the client that waiter used (`Arc::ptr_eq` on its waiters), so a dying client's in-flight waiters cannot kill its replacement. Tests: `a_stalled_control_client_is_killed_and_replaced` (kills mutant MA), `a_stale_waiter_never_clears_the_replacement_client`. |
