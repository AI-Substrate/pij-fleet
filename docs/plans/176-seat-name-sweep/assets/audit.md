## Judgement calls kept on purpose

- Generic animals that share a name with a brand (dove, impala, penguin, falcon, kingfisher, bobcat): the animal is the main sense, and a seat id does not use the mark. jaguar and puma went because the brand is the stronger sense; python and mastodon went in fix round 1 for the same reason in a dev tool.
- Neutral directional or geographic words (northern, southern, eastern, western, continental, overseas, international): no group or judgement attached.
- Positive character words (kind, honest, decent, moral, ethical, loyal, wise): they describe nobody in particular and pair only with animals now.
- Size words for animals (big, giant, huge, large, mammoth, jumbo): fine beside an animal. The weight and body words went.
